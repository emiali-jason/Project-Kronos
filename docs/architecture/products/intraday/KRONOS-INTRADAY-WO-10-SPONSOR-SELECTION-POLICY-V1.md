# WO-10 Sponsor selection

## Current authority — ADR-0042

Status: Approved bounded engineering by direct Sponsor / EA authorization, 2026-09-11.

The earlier permission rules below are superseded prospectively. SELECTED_FUTURE requires independently executable/current authority and positive whole lots; Risk reference, Risk magnitude and monetary Risk availability are advisory. No maximum_permitted_lots is created or enforced. Snapshot-time reference is explicitly bound; stale or superseded reference/economics is reported unavailable at selection without becoming a Risk veto. Above-reference selection records the exact warning and amounts. NONE has null lots and no handoff.

See [ADR-0042](../../adr/ADR-0042-WO10-ADVISORY-RISK-AND-FINAL-FUTURES-COMPOSITION.md) and [advisory Risk policy](KRONOS-INTRADAY-WO-10-ADVISORY-RISK-POLICY-V1.md).

## Preserved predecessor text

**Status:** Approved engineering policy; ADR-0039; version 1.0.0.

SELECTED_FUTURE requires current executable comparison, current Risk APPROVED or
CONSTRAINED, exact positive whole lots at most maximum_permitted_lots, open session
and fresh dynamic evidence. NONE carries null lots and creates no WO11 handoff.
Options are visible NOT_COMMISSIONED_V1, never selectable. Exact replay is
idempotent; conflicting choice or action on an existing comparison is rejected.

Retain comparison, WO09, plan, snapshot, expression, Risk fact/permission, maximum
and Sponsor-selected lots, action identity/time, integrity and programme policy.
Selection creates no fill, position, portfolio exposure, activated quantity,
PAPER/LIVE state, P&L or broker instruction. Full technical evidence remains
available behind the compact Sponsor card. Pros/Cons are versioned structural
facts, never unsupported liquidity labels.

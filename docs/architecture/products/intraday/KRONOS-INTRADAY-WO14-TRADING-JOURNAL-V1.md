# KRONOS Intraday WO-14 Trading Journal V1

**Status:** Sponsor/EA frozen product contract; engineering candidate, not production commissioned.

WO-14 is the Intraday view inside the existing shared Trading Journal. It shows
the opportunity, Sponsor decision, optional track and retained WO-11 outcome.
The population includes PAPER POSITION, PAPER OBSERVATION, NONE, DO NOTHING,
never-entered tracks and terminal outcomes. Observation remains counterfactual
one-lot truth with no portfolio exposure. LIVE is not commissioned.

The book consumes stable origin identity, WO-10 decisions and WO-11 facts. It
never runs Discovery, readiness, trade construction, lifecycle, research,
Provider, monitoring, Telegram or broker operations. Displayed metrics are
retained WO-11 facts; unavailable facts remain blank.

DELETE suppresses one presentation identity persistently. An active lifecycle,
monitoring, Notifications and research history continue. A later unrelated
opportunity has a different origin and is unaffected. V1 has no undelete.

The shared Browser preserves product selection, search, filters, event-time
sorting, drill-down, responsive layout and a healthy empty state. Per-record
monitoring comes from the exact WO-11 owner, never Provider state alone.

UI-BRAND-01 uses local derivatives of the Sponsor master. The shell derivative
preserves the mark, KRONOS wordmark and `by Imran`; compact derivatives serve
favicon and macOS template icon. Branding is cosmetic and local.

WO-12 remains research authority, WO-13 Notifications authority, and future
WO-15 Portfolio and WO-16 Reports are not implemented. Historical WO-14 Risk
Observation authority remains unchanged.

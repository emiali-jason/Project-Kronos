## KRONOS-SWING-PORTFOLIO-STATE-V1

An immutable factual current-state record binds a cycle identity, as-of
boundary, objective-model exposures, Sponsor-position exposures, complete
source identities, provenance, contract version, and integrity SHA. Empty is
valid only when all governed sources were loaded and proved empty. The contract
owns no optimization, Risk permission, quantity, or broker fact.

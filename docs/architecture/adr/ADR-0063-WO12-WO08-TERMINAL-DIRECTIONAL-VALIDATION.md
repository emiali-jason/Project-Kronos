# ADR-0063 — WO12 validation for active WO08

Status: Sponsor-approved bounded engineering and terminal-match policy; exact-byte qualification, independent/owner review and production acceptance are release gates.
Date: 2026-10-11
Owner: EA-Intraday
Authority: WO12-WO08-OPPORTUNITY-EOD-VALIDATION-COMPLETION and direct Sponsor approval of WO12_V1_TERMINAL_DIRECTIONAL_MATCH.

WO08 remains closed and active. This research-only addition does not reopen WO08 or the retirement of new Intraday WO07F. It prospectively supersedes ADR0062's absent EOD/match producer statement; historical publications and associations remain readable with their original bytes.

The validation denominator is a distinct stable opportunity/methodology version that has entered retained WO09 consideration. All98 assessment associations remain an audit, while missing readiness is reported separately. Unavailable/nonadvancing entrants are counted. READY/NOW is not a cohort filter. Missing original assessment or original Assessment Price stays NOT_EVALUABLE; a later assessment, Entry, quote or candle never substitutes for the original.

An immutable consideration pins original run/result, WO08 methodology/checksum, criterion truth/reasons, Native/completed/source identities, stable origin, exact session and MCX contract/roll, original assessment companion and first retained WO09 entry. Subsequent readiness and current-pointer snapshots are separate immutable journey records. They do not infer transitions that upstream persistence did not retain.

During explicit UPDATE RESEARCH only, the existing WO12 application consumes already-retained ordinary Analysis replay envelopes. The endpoint is the exact terminal completed governed 5M close under the frozen DOMAIN008 schedule, including a shortened terminal interval. Both endpoint and retained publication must be available by the evaluation boundary. Exact subject/session and, for MCX, native same-acquisition OHLCV and original exact contract are verified. Original and terminal operation-scoped bindings are retained separately: their IDs may lawfully differ as observation boundaries differ. No continuous/reference series or contract substitution is permitted. NATGAS remains HELD operationally.

Existing WO06H signed movement arithmetic is reused. Positive signed movement is MATCHED; negative and flat are NOT_MATCHED, with flat separately retained. No threshold or PARTIALLY_MATCHED exists. The interpretation is direction at the session endpoint relative to original Assessment Price only. It establishes no profitability, tradability, target achievement, economic usefulness, excursion quality or WO09/trade correctness.

Outcomes are additive, immutable and integrity checked on retention/readback. A later lawful source may complete an unavailable outcome without rewriting it. Conflicting terminal content denies publication. Pinned source envelopes/facts are resolved and verified again before report readback/publication; schema/integrity/storage faults are not analytical non-matches. The existing research transaction, immutable retention, staged XLSX validation and atomic receipt/pointer publication are reused.

Workbook V2/2.2.0 keeps its six sheets and dedicated opportunity_id links. Original prediction, latest assessment, WO09 journey and terminal validation are distinct columns/events. Aggregation exposes considered/evaluated/unavailable counts, retained progression states, withheld outcomes and separate terminal-nonmatch rates at existing 3/5, 4/5 and 5/5 levels. Denominators are explicit, zero denominators remain unavailable and methodology/market/direction/session/exact-contract strata remain separate. Uncommissioned I2-I5 do not become false criteria.

No Provider request, scheduler, notification, readiness/lifecycle mutation, online threshold change or automatic Research update is added. If ordinary retained acquisition evidence does not contain the terminal candle, the outcome remains NOT_EVALUABLE. No new request or clock/window policy is introduced to force availability. Local Provider doubles are rehearsal evidence only. Future acquisition-policy expansion is a separate authority decision.

Explicit UPDATE RESEARCH refreshes original monthly reports for retained
considerations, including a terminal source retained in a later month. Each
monthly workbook retains its own immutable receipt and independent atomic
publication; there is no multi-workbook transaction claim. A failed month stops
the operation; already committed month receipts remain truthful, and a new
explicit update resumes idempotently. No automatic retry or acquisition occurs.

Original authority readback reconstitutes the frozen consideration from exact
retained WO08/Probables/Assessment Price and original schedule sources; a
rehashed method, criterion, source or companion alteration is rejected. Journey
attribution compares each assessment's own methodology tuple. A missing original
assessment cannot borrow another version's WO09 entry. Synthetic future-version
consumer tests do not commission a future producer or criterion.

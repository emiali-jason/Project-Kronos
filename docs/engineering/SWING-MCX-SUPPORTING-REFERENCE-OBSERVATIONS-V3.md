The expected reference ticker remains the trusted binding echo; it is not the observed label. Readable `CLX2026` and `NGX2026` are not declared aliases of `CL1!` or `NG1!`.

Exact known visible-family conflicts reject. The two retained dated labels and existing canonical reference-family descriptions serve only as negative conflict facts: a Natural Gas label cannot qualify a Light Crude panel, and vice versa. They never establish continuous-series membership or MATCHED correspondence. No prefix/month-code inference, fuzzy alias or Provider fallback is introduced. Wrong or unknown venue and wrong timeframe reject when the identity tuple is available. Native identity validation is unchanged.

The genuinely unavailable-reference exception remains available without permitting nulling of readable evidence. Every non-null venue and timeframe must match independently, even if the symbol or another field is unavailable; partial identity cannot hide a visible contradiction. The original closed-field, duplicate-key, ordering, chart revision, request-pair and immutable artifact checks remain intact.

For each unresolved reference timeframe, M2 remains `NOT_COMPARABLE`. M1 remains `UNDETERMINED` with limited coverage; M3 remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Other permissible visible observations remain retained in the reference's units. Supporting divergence or unavailability is not newly made an automatic Energy entry veto. Existing V2 confirmation rules remain conservative; this change does not claim readiness, construct an advisory, or admit a position.

The Analyst may report visible countdowns/forming indications in existing observation/limitation text. It must not certify exchange sessions or machine completed boundaries, and must not add internal provenance or trading-authority fields. Reference Q4 remains `NOT_APPLICABLE / REFERENCE_PERIOD_NOT_GOVERNED`.

# ADR-0033 — Independent visual temporal contradiction evidence

**Status:** Sponsor-approved bounded product decision; engineering candidate, publication pending.
**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Decision owner:** Sponsor / EA, explicit WO-07E temporal correspondence contract correction.
**Scope:** Intraday Review correspondence and successor Question transport only.

## Decision and supersession

This decision supersedes only the exact observed temporal-equality prerequisite
of [ADR-0032](ADR-0032-INTRADAY-CHART-ANALYST-CORRESPONDENCE-SOURCE.md) for newly
commissioned header 1.1.0 evidence. The Analyst remains the independent visual
observer. No second observer, OCR, endpoint reconstruction or production
methodology change is introduced. Historical header 1.0.0 and receipts retain
their original strict comparator and immutable bytes.

Machine authority retains the exact analysis boundary, source candle endpoints,
completion, trading date, canonical exchange session/timezone, calendar,
selection and panel set. Visual authority retains only visible identity,
timeframe, readability, labels, right edge and affirmative contradiction or
insufficiency findings. Machine orientation may guide a comparison; it cannot
populate observed facts. No hidden timestamp is demanded as a pixel observation.

The visual temporal result is CONFIRMED_COMPATIBLE, VISIBLY_CONTRADICTED or
INSUFFICIENT_VISUAL_EVIDENCE. Compatibility requires enough visible context and
explicit supported absence of later/other contradictions and prohibited forming
evidence. Unknown does not pass. Independently supplied exact facts still reject
contradictions. Missing or invalid native machine evidence cannot be replaced by
visual compatibility. Identity, core content and overall correspondence remain
separate required checks.

A live/countdown candle is forming evidence. It must be omitted from presentation
or visibly outside all qualified completed evidence, with explicit exclusion
basis and independently visible same-window date. Later-day/completed future
evidence cannot use this exception. Historical Review plus later live chart is
prohibited. Bar Replay is exceptional, never a normal Sponsor prerequisite.

MCX native contract/source validation remains primary. International reference
context has no invented machine source or calendar; visual compatibility never
upgrades its NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_ESTABLISHED independent correspondence status.

## Evidence and operational limits

Header 1.1.0, NSE transport 2.3.0 and MCX transport 1.3.0 are successors. Older
PDFs, templates, Answers and receipts are not rewritten. New context is bound to
the entire Answer digest and immutable chart revision. No reinterpretation of
old commissioned PDFs is permitted. One Sponsor PDF and exact Answer inbox,
FB02/FB03, Q6/Q9 anchor NOT_ESTABLISHED, identity publication 1.5.0, currentness,
V1 history and all analytical/trading boundaries are preserved.

Qualification uses predeclared representative chart-equivalent drawings and
independent literal findings. Exact temporal fields are null in positive image
cases. This proves deterministic engineering behavior, not empirical Analyst
accuracy. Real Sponsor/Analyst acceptance remains separate. REV002 is preserved
negative evidence and is not retried, repaired or reclassified.

The boundaries are explicitly non-equivalent:

- Discovery ≠ Promotion.
- Qualification match ≠ Probable.
- Probable ≠ Promotion.
- V1 Probable ≠ V2 Probable; each retains its own methodology and replay.
- Review/Chart Ready ≠ visual analysis or Promotion.
- Question Pack Created ≠ Answer imported.
- Combined batch PDF ≠ shared analytical evidence or Answer import.
- Answer imported ≠ reconciled.
- Review Complete ≠ WO-10 reconciliation complete.
- `PROMOTION_READY` ≠ KR-370 `BUY_NOW`, `SELL_NOW`, `BUY_READY` or `SELL_READY`.
- WO-11 downstream eligibility ≠ KR-370 state; it only admits exact lineage to WO-12 evaluation.
- WO-12 KR-370 `BUY_NOW` / `SELL_NOW` = analytical promotion only, not Entry, Risk, Sponsor decision or execution.
- WO-10/WO-11 product publication ≠ Entry.
- Construction ≠ Risk.
- Risk ≠ Entry Timing.
- Entry Timing ≠ Lifecycle.
- Objective Lifecycle ≠ Sponsor Position.

This registry does not invent unfrozen states, transition predicates, or
numerical values.
